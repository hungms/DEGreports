Leading-edge overlap

Per contrast, pathways with padj < 0.05 are pooled across all MSigDB collections. Pairwise Jaccard similarity of leading-edge gene sets is clustered with simplifyEnrichment binary_cut (to choose k). Splits and word clouds cut the same Jaccard hclust dendrogram at that k, so partition lines match the trees. The heatmap omits pathway name labels; side word clouds summarise tokens from pathway names within each cluster.

666 significant pathway(s) (padj < 0.05; all collections pooled). Heatmap shows the top 40 by |NES|; full Jaccard matrix is in the CSV. Binary-cut found 7 cluster(s).
