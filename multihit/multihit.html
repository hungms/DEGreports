Cross-analysis gene hits

Each gene scores +1 per method it is a hit in (DEG, GSEA, VIPER, decoupler-tf, decoupler-ccc, CSPA):

  • DEG: padj < 0.05 and |log2FC| > 0.5
  • CSPA: a DEG (above) that is a CSPA surface protein
  • decoupleR TF: padj < 0.05 and |score| > 1
  • decoupleR CCC: ligand or receptor of a pair with padj < 0.05 and |score| > 1
  • VIPER: padj < 0.05 and |NES| > 2
  • GSEA LE: in any significant pathway leading edge (padj < 0.05)

Method overlap shows how many genes each combination of methods shares; Top genes shows the top 10 up and 10 down genes per contrast (direction from DEG log2FoldChange), ranked by hit count.

Reproduce multi-hit ranking with this package module:

multihit_out <- run_multihit_pipeline(
  diffexp_out, gsea_out = gsea_out,
  viper_out = viper_out, decoupler_tf_out = decoupler_tf_out,
  decoupler_ccc_out = decoupler_ccc_out, cspa_out = cspa_out
)
